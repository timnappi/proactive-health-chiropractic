Care Philosophy

Thoughtful care for the spine, nervous system, and the way you move through life.

Proactive Health Chiropractic brings a calm, thorough approach to chiropractic care. Dr. Nappi works with patients who want to understand what is contributing to discomfort, improve mobility, and support sustainable function through rehabilitation, nutrition, movement, and precise hands-on care.

The practice emphasizes clear evaluation, personalized recommendations, and care that considers posture, movement habits, work demands, activity goals, and day-to-day quality of life.

Services We Provide

Clinical methods that help guide care.

These techniques and movement systems help Dr. Nappi evaluate how you move, identify restrictions, and choose care that fits your goals.

ART

Active Release Techniques

A hands-on soft tissue method that combines precise pressure with patient movement.

FMS

Functional Movement Screen

A movement screen used to observe mobility, stability, and basic movement patterns.

SFMA

Selective Functional Movement Assessment

A clinical movement assessment used when pain is part of the picture.

Graston

Instrument-Assisted Soft Tissue Mobilization

A soft-tissue approach using specialized instruments to address restrictions.

Cupping

Suction-Based Soft Tissue Therapy

A technique that uses controlled suction to work with soft tissue and local circulation.

MDT

McKenzie Method

Mechanical Diagnosis and Therapy for musculoskeletal assessment and self-management.
